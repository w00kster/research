**Category**: Software

**Tags**: project-management, skills, competencies, leadership, methodology

## Overview
A framework or collection of resources focused on the skills and competencies required for effective project management. Covers both technical project management methodologies and the soft skills necessary for leading teams and delivering successful projects.

## Key Points
- **Technical Skills**: Methodologies like Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, and critical path analysis
- **Leadership Abilities**: Team motivation, conflict resolution, and stakeholder management
- **Communication**: Effective reporting, presentation, and facilitation skills
- **Risk Management**: Identification, assessment, and mitigation of project risks
- **Budget & Schedule**: Financial planning, cost control, and timeline management
