# Inbox Zero - Email Productivity and Management

**Category**: Software
**Added**: 2026-08-06
**Status**: Active
**Tags**: email, productivity, inbox, communication, organization

## Overview
Methodology and software tools for achieving and maintaining an empty email inbox. Focuses on processing emails efficiently through systematic handling, filtering, and follow-up strategies to reduce email overload.

## Key Points
- **Processing System**: Handle each email once with clear action (delete, delegate, respond, defer)
- **Automation**: Use filters and rules to automatically sort incoming messages
- **Time Management**: Schedule specific times for email processing to avoid constant interruption
- **Zero Inbox Goal**: Maintain empty or near-empty inbox through disciplined processing
- **Stress Reduction**: Reduce anxiety associated with email overload and fear of missing important messages
