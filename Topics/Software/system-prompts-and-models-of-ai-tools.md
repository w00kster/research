# System Prompts and Models of AI Tools - Understanding AI Behavior

**Category**: Software
**Added**: 2026-08-06
**Status**: Active
**Tags**: ai, prompts, models, artificial-intelligence, llm, behavior

## Overview
A resource focused on understanding and documenting the system prompts and underlying models of various AI tools. Helps users and developers comprehend how AI systems are instructed to behave, what capabilities they have, and their limitations through analysis of their system-level instructions and model architectures.

## Key Points
- **System Prompts Analysis**: Examine the hidden instructions that guide AI behavior
- **Model Architecture**: Understand the underlying AI models powering different tools
- **Behavior Prediction**: Better predict how AI tools will respond in various situations
- **Tool Comparison**: Compare capabilities and limitations across different AI systems
- **Prompt Engineering**: Learn effective techniques for interacting with AI tools based on their design
