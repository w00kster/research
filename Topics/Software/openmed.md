# OpenMed - Open Source Medical Software

**Category**: Software
**Added**: 2026-08-06
**Status**: Active
**Tags**: medical, healthcare, open-source, health-it, emr

## Overview
Open source software solutions for healthcare and medical applications. Includes electronic medical records (EMR), hospital management systems, medical imaging tools, and health information exchange platforms designed to improve healthcare delivery while reducing costs.

## Key Points
- **Cost Effective**: Eliminates licensing fees associated with proprietary medical software
- **Customizable**: Adapt to specific clinical workflows and regional requirements
- **Interoperable**: Support for health information exchange standards (HL7, FHIR, DICOM)
- **Community Driven**: Benefit from collective improvements and security audits
- **Privacy Focused**: Designed with patient data protection and compliance in mind
