# Tildagon Badge - EMF Camp 2024 Hardware Badge

**Category**: Hardware

## Overview
The Tildagon badge is the official electronic badge for EMF Camp 2024. Features an RGB LED display, input buttons, and expansion capabilities for hardware hacking and creative expression at the event.

## Key Points
- **Event Badge**: Official hardware for EMF Camp 2024 attendees
- **Expandable Design**: GPIO pins for adding sensors and peripherals
- **Community Focus**: Designed for workshop participation and badge hacking
- **Open Source**: Fully documented hardware and software available
