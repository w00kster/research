# Battery Emulator - Battery Testing and Simulation

**Category**: Hardware
**Added**: 2026-08-06
**Status**: Active
**Tags**: battery, emulator, testing, electronics, power

## Overview
Hardware and software tools for emulating battery behavior in electronic systems. Enables testing of battery-powered devices under various conditions without consuming actual batteries.

## Key Points
- **Battery Simulation**: Models battery chemistry and behavior under load
- **Test Automation**: Enables repeatable testing of power management systems
- **Safety Testing**: Allows dangerous condition testing without risk
- **Development Aid**: Helps optimize firmware for battery efficiency
