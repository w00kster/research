# Hiring Without Whiteboards - Alternative Technical Interview Practices

**Category**: General
**Added**: 2026-08-06
**Status**: Active
**Tags**: hiring, interviews, recruitment, human-resources, assessment

## Overview
Methodologies and practices for conducting technical interviews without relying on traditional whiteboard coding exercises. Focuses on assessing candidates' abilities through more realistic, job-relevant tasks and evaluations that better predict actual job performance.

## Key Points
- **Practical Assessment**: Evaluate skills through real-world coding tasks and projects
- **Reduced Anxiety**: Eliminate the stress and artificiality of whiteboard interviews
- **Better Predictors**: Job simulations and take-home projects predict performance more accurately
- **Inclusive Practices**: Reduce bias and create fairer evaluation processes
- **Skill-Based Focus**: Assess actual abilities rather than interview performance under pressure
